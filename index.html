01 — About

The Man
Behind the Lens

I am Joshua Ojemoron — a Lagos-based cinematographer and visual storyteller operating under the brand Whaleshot.

My foundation started in church media, where I built a sharp eye for storytelling and real-time technical discipline. I later refined my craft through professional training at Praxis Digital and hands-on set experience across music videos, short films, and commercial productions.

I've worked across roles as cinematographer, assistant director, editor, and gaffer — giving me a complete understanding of production from concept to final delivery. I shoot on Sony FX6, FX3, and Drone. I don't just shoot videos — I craft visuals that connect.
